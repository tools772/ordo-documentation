# Where the name Ordo comes from

**Ordo** is a Latin word. It primarily means **order**, **row**, **series**, or **methodical arrangement**.

That root is the foundation of ideas we still use in English — including the modern word **system**: a set of parts arranged so they work together.

---

## Why that name for this product

Insurance payment posting is a pipeline of ordered steps:

1. The remittance arrives.
2. Patients and procedure lines are read from the page.
3. Someone matches them to the Open Dental chart.
4. Someone posts the payment.
5. Someone can later audit who did what.

Ordo is the desk that keeps that work in **order** — a clear row of files, a series of statuses, and a methodical path from upload to post. The name is a nod to that arrangement, not to replacing Open Dental as your practice management system.
