# Reports has moved

**Reports** is now the **Overview** tab of **Posting Analytics**. Open it from the sidebar: **Analytics → Posting Analytics → Overview**. Old bookmarks to Reports open that tab automatically.

The numbers, filters (uploaded date and EOBs), and export are the same. The page now starts idle — click **Load overview** to see the numbers.
